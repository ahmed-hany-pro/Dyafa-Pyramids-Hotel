Our Services

Explore Our Services

Rooms & Bedroom

Linens, wardrobe or closet, alarm clock, soundproof rooms, air conditioning, heating, and hardwood or parquet floors for a restful stay.
Bathroom Amenities

Private bathroom with toilet, bidet, shower, towels, slippers, hairdryer, and complimentary free toiletries for your comfort.
Food & Drink

Restaurant, bar, coffee house, snack bar, room service, minibar, breakfast in the room, kid-friendly buffet, and special diet meals on request.
Indoor Swimming Pool

Free indoor pool open all year, welcoming all ages. Features a plunge pool, shallow end, pool bar, beach chairs/loungers, and pool/beach towels.
Spa & Wellness

Full-body, couples, foot, head, back, neck, and hand massages. Spa/wellness packages, spa lounge, relaxation area, public bath, and massage chairs.
Activities & Entertainment

Bicycle rental, bike tours, walking tours, diving, cycling, live music, evening entertainment, themed dinners, and local culture tours.
Family Friendly

Family rooms, kids' meals, indoor play area, outdoor play equipment, snorkeling, horseback riding, and a pool table for the whole family.
Outdoors & Garden

Terrace, garden, picnic area, outdoor furniture, and an outdoor fireplace — perfect for relaxing in a serene open-air setting.
Front Desk & Concierge

24-hour front desk, concierge, express check-in/out, baggage storage, tour desk, currency exchange, ATM on site, and lockers.
Transport & Parking

Free private parking on site, airport shuttle, car rental, public transit tickets, and shuttle service available for your travel convenience.
Cleaning & Laundry

Daily housekeeping, suit press, ironing service, dry cleaning, and laundry services to keep you fresh and well-presented throughout your stay.
Safety & Business

24-hour security, CCTV, smoke alarms, fire extinguishers, safe in room, plus meeting/banquet facilities and business services for professionals.
