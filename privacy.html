Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. This page is a general template — please have it reviewed by a legal adviser before relying on it.

Information we collect

When you make a booking, send a message, subscribe to updates or place a guest-services request, we collect the details you give us, such as your name, email address, phone number, stay dates and special requests.

How we use it

We use your details to confirm and manage your stay, answer your enquiries, fulfil room-service, laundry, housekeeping and tour requests, and (only if you subscribe) send hotel updates. We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies & local storage

This website stores a small amount of data in your browser, such as your light/dark theme choice and the items in your guest-services order, so the site works the way you left it. It does not use this data to identify you. You can clear it at any time from your browser settings.

Your choices
